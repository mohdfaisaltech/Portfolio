COMMON QUESTIONS

Booking and service information

How can I book an AC technician in Hyderabad?

Call +91 85200 52291 or contact Cooling Expert on WhatsApp. Share your locality, AC type and the problem. The team can discuss the service requirement and confirm appointment availability.

Do you provide same-day AC service in Hyderabad?

Same-day service is available across Hyderabad, subject to technician availability and appointment confirmation. Contact us with your locality and preferred time to check availability.

Do you provide AC service outside Hyderabad?

Service in other Telangana cities is considered by prior appointment. Please contact us before booking so travel and availability can be discussed and confirmed.

Which AC brands do you service?

Cooling Expert works on AC systems from brands including LG, Daikin, Blue Star, Carrier, Godrej, O General, Mitsubishi, Videocon and Onida. Contact us with your model details to discuss the specific repair.

How much does AC servicing cost?

Published rates depend on the AC type and service required. For example, split AC normal wet service is ₹399 and split AC jet pump deep service is ₹599. See the complete AC service price list for other listed work.

Is there an inspection or fault-checking charge?

Testing and fault inspection is ₹300. If the repair is completed, this amount is adjustable in the final bill. Any repair work and its cost should be discussed before proceeding.

How much does AC gas charging cost?

The published complete gas-charging rates are R-22: ₹2,199, R-32: ₹2,399 and R-410A: ₹2,699. The required refrigerant and work should be confirmed after checking the AC.

Do you repair inverter AC PCBs?

Yes. The listed inverter split PCB repair package is ₹3,500 and includes removal, servicing and installation. The listed PCB repair warranty is 3 months.

What warranty do you provide?

A 3-month warranty applies to the PCB repair work specified in the price list. This warranty does not automatically apply to AC servicing, gas charging, installation or other services.

Do you provide AC installation and uninstallation?

Yes. Installation, re-installation and dismantling are listed services. Charges depend on the AC type and the specific work required. Refer to the pricing page for published rates.

Do you repair AC gas leaks and copper piping?

Leak inspection and brazing, along with copper piping work, are among the listed services. The required materials, measurements and final scope should be checked before a quote is confirmed.

What information should I share when requesting a quote?

Share your name, Hyderabad locality, AC brand and type, a short description of the issue and your preferred appointment time. This helps the team understand the job before confirming a visit.
